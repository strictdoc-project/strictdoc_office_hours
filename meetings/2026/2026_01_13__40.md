## SPDX implementation plan

SPDX serialization formats are based on RDF: [https://spdx.github.io/spdx-spec/v3.0.1/serializations/](https://spdx.github.io/spdx-spec/v3.0.1/serializations/). Key change: disregard [https://github.com/spdx/tools-python](https://github.com/spdx/tools-python), switch to the lower-level [https://github.com/RDFLib/rdflib](https://github.com/RDFLib/rdflib).
