## SPDX conformance example

Last time Greg walked us through the content of the SPDX conformance example: https://github.com/gregshue/spdx-examples/tree/conformance-example1/conformance Now we could discuss the mapping of this content to SPDX entities as well as the overall structure of this example as a CRA project template. Considering Need as an SPDX entity: Need has a similar structure like Requirement. Need's Rationale is not a mandatory field. Needs are from Problem space, not Solution Space. Needs are NEVER in Solution Space. From SEBoK Wiki Glossary: stakeholder needs: Stakeholder needs include elicitation of needs, wants, desires, expectations and perceived constraints of identified stakeholders. Needs concentrate on system purpose and behavior, and are described in the context of the operational environment and conditions. (**our comment: helps to identify the scope)** (ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288, 2023, Section 6.4.2) Before Requirements are created, some work has to be done before to capture the customer needs. German word: Lastenheft vs Pflichtenheft. Can we say that Needs \== Lastenheft. Here is the first part of SEBoK Wiki entry for stakeholder requirement: Stakeholder requirements are transformed from the stakeholder needs to objectively adequate, structured and more formal statements. (ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148, 2018, Section 5.2.3) Needs -\> Stakeholder requirements -\> System requirements -\> HLR -\> LLR.
