## KiCad discussion (summary)

- Steve is preparing his proposal, **StrictDoc–KiCad Experimental Integration**.
- He wants to gather feedback from the StrictDoc community as well as by
  contacting people from the KiCad community.
- Once the proposal reaches sufficient maturity, Steve will share it with the
  KiCad community.
- The proposal includes references to Altium/Valispace as inspiration for what
  could be implemented for KiCad/StrictDoc.
- To implement requirements support in KiCad, there are three options, ranging
  from a KiCad plugin (the least invasive option) to full native requirements
  support in KiCad.
- Stanislav suggested that the proposal could introduce the StrictDoc Markdown
  format as a format that KiCad could parse to read requirements data stored in
  Git-based text files.
- Tobias suggested that KiCad could read requirements directly from the same
  requirements repository instead of creating its own copy of the requirements,
  for example in its own requirements format or a database. We discussed that
  both the repository organization and the requirements storage format depend on
  the workflows used by KiCad users. Understanding these use cases is one of the
  main topics we would like to discuss with a broader audience.
- In response to a question from Ryan: StrictDoc supports traceability between
  requirements and source files. In theory, StrictDoc's `@relation` markers
  could be added to KiCad source files to provide traceability to requirements.
  The KiCad user interface could also allow users to connect requirements to
  schematic artifacts while using `@relation` markers for traceability under the
  hood.
